How PawPrint AI Works

  1. Upload a clear footprint photo (JPG / PNG).
  2. Our EfficientNetV2 model classifies the species.
  3. OpenCV estimates footprint area to infer age group.
  4. We map species to wildness and human risk levels.

Tip: crop the image so the footprint fills most of the frame for best results.
